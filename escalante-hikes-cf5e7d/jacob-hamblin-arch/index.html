Jacob Hamblin Arch Guided Day Hike

Coyote Gulch, in a day

Kind of day: Big day, hardy terrain

Jacob Hamblin Arch rises out of the bottom of Coyote Gulch, the size of a small country church, with a ribbon of water running underneath it. It's the kind of thing people who have spent a lifetime in canyon country still go quiet under. The photos never get the scale. The scale only lands when you're standing under it.

Most people see it on a backpacking trip. We also walk it in a day: a non-technical route from Chimney Rock, across open desert and down into the gulch. It's 12 miles on hardy terrain, and it's a full day.

The hike in brief. Jacob Hamblin Arch is a guided day hike into Coyote Gulch, a side canyon of the Escalante River in southern Utah. We walk 12 miles round trip from Chimney Rock, off Hole-in-the-Rock Road, on a non-technical route. It's a full day from the town of Escalante. You can do it as one day of our five-day Escalante Extravaganza in spring or fall, or as part of a private Escalante trip of three to five days.

If you hike regularly and 12 miles in a day doesn't scare you, yes.

Where you're headed

Coyote Gulch is a side canyon of the Escalante River, far down Hole-in-the-Rock Road.

We meet in the town of Escalante, on Scenic Byway 12, and drive down Hole-in-the-Rock Road to Chimney Rock. From there we walk across the desert and down into Hurricane Wash, follow the wash to Coyote Gulch, and walk the gulch to the arch.

Questions guests ask

Can you see Jacob Hamblin Arch in one day?

Yes. We walk to it and back in a day from Chimney Rock, 12 miles round trip on a non-technical route.

Is the day hike to Jacob Hamblin Arch technical?

No. Our route from Chimney Rock is non-technical. There are shorter ways into Coyote Gulch that involve steep, exposed slickrock. We don't use them*.

Would I be better off backpacking Coyote Gulch?

If you have four days, maybe. The backpacking trip goes on past the arch to Coyote Natural Bridge, Cliff Arch and the Escalante River, and you camp in the canyon. The day hike gets you to the arch and back. Here's the backpacking trip.

What happens in bad weather?

Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

Is lunch included?

No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch, or ask us about adding one of ours*.

How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12 in southern Utah, and you'll need a car to reach it. The nearest big airports are Las Vegas and Salt Lake City. By road it's about 315 miles from Las Vegas, 285 from Salt Lake City and 270 from Grand Junction, each four and a half to five hours of driving, and 120 miles from Kanab, about two and a half hours*.

Every hike starts and finishes in town. Lodging isn't part of our price. You book your own place to stay: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around Escalante, and they fill up in spring and fall.

What does it cost to cancel?

Our day tour terms apply*. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page.
