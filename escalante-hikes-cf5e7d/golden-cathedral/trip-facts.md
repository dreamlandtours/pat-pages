## In brief
The Golden Cathedral is a guided day hike in the Escalante canyons of southern Utah. We walk about 10 miles round trip from the Egypt trailhead: down slickrock to the Escalante River, across the river several times, and up Neon Canyon to the alcove. It's a 10 to 12 hour day from the town of Escalante, drive included. You can do it as one day of our five-day Escalante Extravaganza in spring or fall, or as part of a private Escalante trip of three to five days.

## Facts
- Distance: About 10 miles round trip
- The day: 10 to 12 hours from Escalante, drive included
- Underfoot: Slickrock, sand and river cobbles
- The river: Several wades, ankle to thigh deep, and cold
- Starts in: Escalante, Utah
- Group: Ten guests at most, with two guides
- Kind of day: Strenuous. The toughest of our Escalante days.

## Who it suits
- About 10 miles on slickrock, sand and river cobbles. The day opens with a steep slickrock descent off the Egypt bench, and that same slope is your climb out at the end, when your legs are tired.
- You'll wade the Escalante River several times. The crossings are wades, not swims: feet and shins mostly, sometimes up to your thighs. In spring and late fall that water is cold.
- It isn't technical. No ropes and no swimming. The canyoneers you may see rappelling into the Cathedral come in from above; we walk the canyon floor to the pool.
- There are no facilities once we leave the trucks, and it's a long way from anywhere.
- With the cold water and the miles, it's a day for adults and strong older teens.

## What to bring
- Shoes or boots you don't mind soaking, plus dry socks for after
- Layers. Mornings can be near freezing in early spring and late fall, and the river is cold.
- A daypack, and a dry bag for your camera or phone on the crossings
- Trekking poles. They help on the crossings and on the slickrock
- Your lunch

## Questions
**How deep is the river on the Golden Cathedral hike?** Usually somewhere between ankle and thigh deep. The crossings are wades, not swims, so it's mostly feet and shins that get wet. The Escalante can rise with rain, and cold water plus cold air is no joke, so your guides decide on the day whether the river is safe to cross.

**Do I need ropes or canyoneering skills?** No. The deep water at the Cathedral is where canyoneers rappel in from above. Coming in on foot the way we do, you walk the canyon floor to the pool at the base of the alcove.

**How hard is the Golden Cathedral hike?** It's strenuous: about 10 miles, a steep slickrock descent and climb, several cold river wades, and a 10 to 12 hour day from Escalante. It's the toughest of our five Escalante days, a notch harder than the Cosmic Ashtray.

**When is the light best in the Golden Cathedral?** The alcove glows when late-morning and midday light bounces off the pool, and that's when we're there. Bring a way to keep your camera dry on the crossings.

**Can I do this hike without a guide?** Yes. Strong, experienced desert hikers with a real 4x4 do. What you get with us is the drive down a rough road, the route, somebody who makes the river call, and guides who can tell you what you're looking at.

**Can I bring a drone?** No. Please leave it at home for this one.

**What happens in bad weather?** Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

**Is lunch included?** No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch, or ask us about adding one of ours.

**How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?** Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12, about a three-hour drive from Kanab. Most guests drive or rent a car. Every hike starts and finishes in town, so you base yourself there for the days you join us. Lodging is yours to book: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around town, and they fill up in spring and fall.

**What does it cost to cancel?** Our day tour terms apply. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page .
