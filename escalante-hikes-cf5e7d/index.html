Guided Hiking in Escalante, Utah

Five day hikes, two ways to go

Every time we go deeper into the Escalante canyons, we come home thinking the same thing: we know a lot of people who'd love it here. Slickrock in every direction. A river you wade. Slot canyons, a sandstone cathedral, a bowl of orange sand the wind dug out of white rock.

It sits just far enough east of Kanab that we usually can't run day tours there, because the drive alone eats the day. So we go for days at a time, and you base yourself in the town of Escalante.

In brief. Dreamland Safari Tours guides five day hikes out of Escalante, Utah: the Golden Cathedral, the Cosmic Ashtray, Jacob Hamblin Arch, Peekaboo and Spooky slot canyons, and Zebra and Tunnel slot canyons. There are two ways to do them. The Escalante Extravaganza is a scheduled week in spring and fall, five hikes in five days, from $269 per person per day. A private trip is three to five days for your own group, from $1,575 per group per day.

Where it is

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12 in southern Utah, between Bryce Canyon and Capitol Reef. Every hike starts and finishes there.

Four of the five trailheads are off Hole-in-the-Rock Road, the dirt road that runs southeast from town. Your guides drive it. You don't need a 4x4 to join us, only a way to get to Escalante: by road it's about 315 miles from Las Vegas, 285 from Salt Lake City and 270 from Grand Junction, each four and a half to five hours of driving, and 120 miles from Kanab, about two and a half hours*.

Questions guests ask

How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12 in southern Utah, and you'll need a car to reach it. The nearest big airports are Las Vegas and Salt Lake City. By road it's about 315 miles from Las Vegas, 285 from Salt Lake City and 270 from Grand Junction, each four and a half to five hours of driving, and 120 miles from Kanab, about two and a half hours*.

Every hike starts and finishes in town. Lodging isn't part of our price. You book your own place to stay: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around Escalante, and they fill up in spring and fall.

How hard are these hikes?

Harder than most of what we guide around Kanab. The three big days run 8 to 12 miles and fill the whole day. The two slot canyon days are 4 to 7 miles and about six hours, and they're still physical. Each hike's page says plainly who it suits.

Which hike should I pick if I only have one day?

For the place nobody forgets, the Golden Cathedral, if you're fit and don't mind cold wading. For something no photo prepares you for, the Cosmic Ashtray. For slot canyons, Peekaboo and Spooky*.

What happens in bad weather?

Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

Is lunch included?

No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch, or ask us about adding one of ours*.

What does it cost to cancel?

Our day tour terms apply*. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page.
