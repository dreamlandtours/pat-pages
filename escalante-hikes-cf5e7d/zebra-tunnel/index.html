Zebra and Tunnel Slot Canyons Guided Hike

Harris Wash, near Escalante

Kind of day: Lighter day, still physical

Zebra Slot is aptly named. The walls are striped pink and white from floor to sky, and they close in until you're walking sideways.*

Tunnel, its neighbor, is the other half of the day. Together they make the lighter kind of Escalante day, and it's still physical.

The hike in brief. Zebra and Tunnel are two slot canyons near Escalante in southern Utah. Our guided hike covers 7 miles and takes about 6 hours. It's the closing day of our five-day Escalante Extravaganza in spring and fall, and you can also make it part of a private Escalante trip of three to five days.

Is this hike for you?

If you're active and don't mind tight spaces or wet feet, yes.

  • It's in the same range as Peekaboo and Spooky: lighter than our three big Escalante days, more demanding than most of what we guide around Kanab.
  • Slot canyons mean narrow passages*. If small spaces aren't your thing, tell us before you book.
  • Slots are the first thing we change when rain is in the forecast.

Want a bigger day? Look at the Cosmic Ashtray, Jacob Hamblin Arch or the Golden Cathedral.

Questions guests ask

Is there water in Zebra Slot?

Often*. How much changes with the season and the last rain, and your guides check before the day.

What happens in bad weather?

Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

Is lunch included?

No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch, or ask us about adding one of ours*.

How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12, about a three-hour drive from Kanab*. Most guests drive or rent a car. Every hike starts and finishes in town, so you base yourself there for the days you join us. Lodging is yours to book: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around town, and they fill up in spring and fall.

What does it cost to cancel?

Our day tour terms apply*. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page.
