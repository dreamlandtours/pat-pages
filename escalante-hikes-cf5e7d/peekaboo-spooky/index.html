Peekaboo and Spooky Slot Canyons Guided Hike

Dry Fork, near Escalante

Kind of day: Lighter day, still physical

Peekaboo and Spooky are the slot-canyon pair people come to Escalante for: two narrow, twisting canyons a short walk apart, off the Dry Fork of Coyote Gulch.*

It's the lighter kind of Escalante day, and it's still physical. Don't confuse it with the Peekaboo slot we guide near Kanab. Nothing on our Escalante list is Peekaboo-Kanab easy.

The hike in brief. Peekaboo and Spooky are two slot canyons at Dry Fork, down Hole-in-the-Rock Road from Escalante in southern Utah. Our guided hike covers 4 to 6 miles and takes about 6 hours. It's the opening day of our five-day Escalante Extravaganza in spring and fall, and you can also make it part of a private Escalante trip of three to five days.

Is this hike for you?

If you're active and you like the idea of squeezing through a canyon, yes.

Want a bigger day? Look at the Cosmic Ashtray, Jacob Hamblin Arch or the Golden Cathedral. After the Kanab one? That's our Peekaboo Slot Canyon tour*.

Questions guests ask

Is this the same Peekaboo as your Kanab tour?

No. There are two Peekaboo slot canyons in southern Utah. Our half-day Peekaboo tour is the one near Kanab. This page is about the one at Dry Fork, near Escalante, which is a bigger and more physical day.

How tight is Spooky?

Tight*. If you're unsure about small spaces, call us before you book and we'll tell you straight whether it's for you.

What happens in bad weather?

Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

Is lunch included?

No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch, or ask us about adding one of ours*.

How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12 in southern Utah, and you'll need a car to reach it. The nearest big airports are Las Vegas and Salt Lake City. By road it's about 315 miles from Las Vegas, 285 from Salt Lake City and 270 from Grand Junction, each four and a half to five hours of driving, and 120 miles from Kanab, about two and a half hours*.

Every hike starts and finishes in town. Lodging isn't part of our price. You book your own place to stay: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around Escalante, and they fill up in spring and fall.

What does it cost to cancel?

Our day tour terms apply*. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page.
