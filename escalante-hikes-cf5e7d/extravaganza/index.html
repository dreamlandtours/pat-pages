Escalante hikes

Escalante Extravaganza

Five days, five hikes, one base in Escalante

The slickrock country around the town of Escalante sits just far enough east of Kanab that we usually can't run day tours there: the drive alone eats the day. So twice a year we pack the trucks and move in for five days.

To be honest about what this is: big hikes, particularly the three in the middle. Not punishing, but definitely earned. The slot canyon days are lighter, and they're still physical. All of it is the kind of country people fly across continents to see.

The week in brief. The Escalante Extravaganza is five guided day hikes in five days out of the town of Escalante, Utah, run once in spring and once in fall. Each day is one hike: Peekaboo and Spooky slot canyons, the Golden Cathedral, the Cosmic Ashtray, Jacob Hamblin Arch, and Zebra and Tunnel slot canyons. Come for all five days or pick the ones you want. You stay in Escalante, and we drive you to each trailhead and back.

Why spring and fall

These are the seasons when the canyons are at their best for walking: cool mornings, comfortable afternoons, and in early November the cottonwoods along the river turn gold. Summer is too hot for the big days, and the late-summer storms are no time to be in a slot canyon.

Questions guests ask

Do I have to come for all five days?

No. Come for all five, or pick the days you want. Three or more days are $269 each. For one or two days, a slot canyon day is $269 and a big hiking day is $299.

Can the order of the hikes change?

Yes. The first and the last day stay put. The three big days in the middle can trade places when the weather asks for it, and we tell you the evening before. If you've booked single days, we'll move you with your hike.

How fit do I need to be for the whole week?

Fit enough to walk 40-odd miles in five days, three of them long. The week opens and closes with a lighter day. If you hike regularly, you'll be tired and happy by Monday.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. On the Extravaganza you join a group of up to ten, and plenty of guests come solo. You pay the normal per-person price.

What happens in bad weather?

Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

Is lunch included?

No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch.

How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12 in southern Utah, and you'll need a car to reach it. The nearest big airports are Las Vegas and Salt Lake City. By road it's about 315 miles from Las Vegas, 285 from Salt Lake City and 270 from Grand Junction, each four and a half to five hours of driving, and 120 miles from Kanab, about two and a half hours.

Every hike starts and finishes in town. Lodging isn't part of our price. You book your own place to stay: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around Escalante, and they fill up in spring and fall.

What does it cost to cancel?

Our day tour terms apply. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page.
