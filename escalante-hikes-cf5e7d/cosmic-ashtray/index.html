Cosmic Ashtray Guided Day Hike

Off-trail slickrock near Escalante

Kind of day: Challenging

People ask us about the Cosmic Ashtray all the time. It's a wind-carved pit about two hundred feet across and sixty deep, ringed by smooth white sandstone domes, with a deep pool of burnt-orange sand at the bottom and a rock tower standing dead center. Geologists call it a weathering pit. Most days, you'll have it to yourselves.

There's no marked path in. The whole day is off-trail, over bare slickrock and soft sand, open to the sun, with a lot of route-finding. That's the norm out here, and it's a big part of the fun.

The hike in brief. The Cosmic Ashtray is a guided day hike near Escalante in southern Utah. We walk about 8 miles round trip, off-trail over slickrock and sand, about three hours each way, with lunch at the rim. It's about a 9 hour day from the town of Escalante, drive included. You can do it as one day of our five-day Escalante Extravaganza in spring or fall, or as part of a private Escalante trip of three to five days.

Is this hike for you?

If you're up for an active day and happy to put in the miles, you'll fit right in.

  • About 8 miles over bare slickrock and soft sand, with a steep sandy drop down to the rim near the end, which you climb back up afterward.
  • It's remote and open to the sun the whole way. Your guides carry plenty of water and set a steady pace.
  • There's no water source and there are no facilities once we leave the trucks. It's a real backcountry day.
  • It's a long, demanding day, so it's one for adults and older teens.

This is the most forgiving of our three big Escalante days*. For more, look at the Golden Cathedral. For less, look at Peekaboo and Spooky or Zebra and Tunnel. Not sure? Call us at (801) 251-6036.

Where you're headed

The Ashtray sits in open slickrock country east of the town of Escalante, above the canyons of the Escalante River.

We meet in Escalante, on Scenic Byway 12. The drive is 35 to 40 minutes: a short paved stretch on Highway 12, then a rough dirt road the trucks are built for. From there we walk.

Questions guests ask

What is the Cosmic Ashtray?

A weathering pit: a bowl about two hundred feet across and sixty deep that the wind has carved into white sandstone, with a floor of burnt-orange sand and a rock tower in the middle. You'll also see it called the Cosmic Navel.

Is there a trail to the Cosmic Ashtray?

No. There's no marked path. It's off-trail slickrock and sand with a lot of route-finding, which is why most people who ask us about it want a guide.

How hard is the Cosmic Ashtray hike?

It's challenging: about 8 miles round trip, roughly three hours each way, all of it in the open sun with no water along the way.

Do we go down into the bowl?

Plan on the rim*. That's where the view is.

What happens in bad weather?

Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

Is lunch included?

No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch, or ask us about adding one of ours*.

How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12, about a three-hour drive from Kanab*. Most guests drive or rent a car. Every hike starts and finishes in town, so you base yourself there for the days you join us. Lodging is yours to book: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around town, and they fill up in spring and fall.

What does it cost to cancel?

Our day tour terms apply*. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page.
