Escalante hikes

Private Escalante Hiking Trip

Your dates, your group, your pick of the hikes

Some trips are better with just your own people: a family, two couples, the friends you've been promising a desert trip for years. Pick three, four or five days, pick your hikes, and you get a Dreamland guide and truck to yourselves in the canyon country around Escalante.

You set the pace. If the group wants an hour under the Golden Cathedral's skylights, you take the hour.

The trip in brief. A private Escalante trip is three to five days of guided day hiking out of the town of Escalante, Utah, for your group alone. You choose from five hikes: Peekaboo and Spooky slot canyons, the Golden Cathedral, the Cosmic Ashtray, Jacob Hamblin Arch, and Zebra and Tunnel slot canyons. The price is per group per day, for up to five people. You stay in Escalante, and your guide drives you to each trailhead and back.

How it works

  1. Call or write with your dates, your group and the hikes you have your eye on.
  2. We put the days in an order that works, with a lighter day first and last.
  3. You book your lodging in Escalante. We meet you there on the first morning.
  4. Your guide drives, leads and makes the weather calls. You walk.

In the price

  • Your own Dreamland guide and truck, for up to five people
  • The drive from Escalante to each trailhead and back
  • Snacks and plenty of water
  • A first-aid kit and emergency communication

Lunch*, lodging, tips, sales tax and public land use fees aren't included.

Questions guests ask

How many people can come on a private trip?

Up to five on one guide and one truck. A sixth person adds $269 a day. From seven people you travel with two guides and two trucks, and the price doubles. Ten is the most we can take as one group*.

Is there a minimum number of days?

Three. The guide and the truck come over from Kanab for your trip, and three days is what makes that drive worth it for both of us.

We're only two. Is a private trip the right choice?

Maybe not. A couple pays the same as a party of five, so it comes to about $812 each a day. On the Extravaganza the same hikes are $269 to $299 each. If your dates are flexible, that's the better deal, and we'll say so.

Can we choose any of the five hikes?

Yes. Most groups open and close with a slot canyon day and put the big hikes in between, because your guide drives over from Kanab on the first morning and home on the last afternoon. We'll build the order with you.

When can we go?

Spring and fall are the seasons for these hikes. Tell us your dates and we'll tell you straight what the weather and the river are likely to be doing.

What happens in bad weather?

Slot canyons, the river and the dirt roads all answer to the weather, and we make the weather calls. If conditions aren't safe, your guides will change the plan and find you something just as good. A change of plan for weather or safety isn't a reason for a refund. If we cancel your day, you get a full refund.

Is lunch included?

No. Snacks and plenty of water are on us. Bring your own lunch, or ask us about adding one of ours*.

How do I get to Escalante, and where do I stay?

Escalante is a small town on Scenic Byway 12, about a three-hour drive from Kanab*. Most guests drive or rent a car. Every hike starts and finishes in town, so you base yourself there for the days you join us. Lodging is yours to book: there are lodges, motels, cabins and campgrounds in and around town, and they fill up in spring and fall.

What does it cost to cancel?

Our day tour terms apply*. Cancel 30 days or more before your hike and the fee is 15%, or you can take a credit toward a future tour. From 11 to 30 days out it's 25%, from 10 days to 48 hours it's 35%, and inside 48 hours or for a no-show it's 100%. The full wording is on our policies page.
