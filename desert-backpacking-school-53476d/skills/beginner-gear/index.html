What gear do I need to start backpacking?

Camp on a Dreamland backpacking trip

You need seven kinds of gear: a backpack, a shelter, a sleep system (sleeping bag and pad), a food and water kit, navigation and emergency items, clothing layers, and footwear. Three of them, the backpack, the shelter and the sleep system, decide most of your comfort, so put your attention there. You don't need to spend a fortune or master every outdoor skill before your first night out.

What are the seven kinds of gear?

We call them the Seven to Thrive. Strip a backpacking kit down and these are the categories left.

CategoryWhat it isFor a desert trip
1. Carrying systemYour backpack55 to 65 liters is the size we ask guests to bring for a four-day Coyote Gulch trip.
2. ShelterA tent, tarp or bivyIn a stable, dry forecast you can leave it behind and sleep in the open.
3. Sleep systemSleeping bag and sleeping padDesert nights are cold in every season. The pad matters as much as the bag.
4. Food and waterStove, cookware, water treatment3 liters of water-carrying capacity per person.
5. Navigation and emergencyGPS, map, first aidThere is no cell service in most of the places we go. Our guides carry a satellite messenger.
6. Clothing layersOne hiking outfit, one sleep layer, a warm jacket, a rain jacketA long-sleeve sun hoodie is the best single clothing purchase for a desert trip.
7. FootwearHiking boots or trail shoesSynthetic and not waterproof if the route has water. See shoes for water hiking.

What are the Big Three, and what should I buy?

The Big Three are your backpack, your shelter and your sleep system. For each we name a value pick for a first kit and an ultralight pick. Ultralight gear isn't necessary to get started. We include it so you know where spending more is worth it. Between them, our guides have spent a couple of thousand nights relying on backpacking gear.

1. Backpack

The pack has to fit you, spread the weight evenly and hold all your gear without extra bulk.

  • Value: Fjällräven Keb 52. An adjustable suspension, plenty of storage and a comfortable hip belt. It takes a hydration bladder.
  • Ultralight: Hyperlite Mountain Gear Southwest 55. Under two pounds, made of Dyneema fabric, and built to carry light loads efficiently while keeping gear dry.

2. Shelter

Your shelter protects you from wind, rain and bugs.

  • Value: Kelty Discovery Trail 1. Free-standing, easy to set up, 3 pounds 6 ounces. This is the tent we provide on our guided trips.
  • Ultralight: Hyperlite Mid-1. A pyramid tent that pitches with a single trekking pole and weighs 16.8 ounces. It's the sports car of backpacking shelters. Make sure you know how to drive it.

3. Sleep system

  • Sleeping bag, value: Kelty Cosmic Down 20. Down insulation at an entry-level price. The 20-degree rating is warm enough for cool desert nights in early spring and late fall.
  • Sleeping bag, ultralight: Hyperlite Mountain Gear 20-Degree Quilt. A quilt has no zipper, which saves almost a full pound against the bag above.
  • Sleeping pads: carry two, one foam and one inflatable. Foam alone is uncomfortable, most of all for side sleepers. An inflatable alone can fail in the field and leave you with no pad, which on a cold night is a safety problem and not only a comfort problem. Our combination: a Therm-a-Rest Z Lite Sol foam pad under a Kelty Mistral SI (value) or a Nemo Tensor Extreme Ultralight Insulated (ultralight).

What do first-timers pack that they should leave at home?

First-timers pack out of anxiety, which is natural and adds pounds. At our gear checks, the things that come out of packs include extra shirts, jeans, a second book, full-size shampoo and a camp chair. If your answer to "what is this for?" is "I don't know, in case", leave it behind. Every pound you don't carry is a pound of enjoyment back.

Can I try gear before I buy it?

Yes, and for a first trip that's the sensible order. On our guided trips we provide the tent and trekking poles, and we can supply a backpack, sleeping bag and sleeping pad if you don't own them. You can also carry a Hyperlite pack on a trip with us, if you want a multi-day test before you buy one.
