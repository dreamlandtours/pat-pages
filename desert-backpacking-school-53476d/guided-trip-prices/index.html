What a guided desert backpacking trip costs

We put eight routes in southern Utah and the Grand Canyon side by side: every outfitter that publishes a price, in dollars per person for each day on the trail.

The short answer

At list price, a guided backpacking trip on these routes costs between $270 and $648 per person for each day on the trail. The highest figure on this page is our own Rim to Rim trip.

Three things explain most of the spread: how many days the trip spends on the trail, what gear and food the price covers, and whether hotel nights are in the price. The tables below show all three beside every price.

We are Dreamland Safari Tours, a guide company in Kanab, Utah. Our own trips sit in these tables next to everyone else's. On some routes we cost less per trail day, and on some we cost more. Both kinds of row are here.

All prices were read on November 24, 2026. Each one links to the page we read it on.

Three kinds of guided trip

Guide and group gear

The price covers a guide, permits, group camping gear and most or all trail meals. You bring your own backpack, sleeping bag and sleeping pad.

On this page: Backcountry Found and Epic Trail Adventures, at $270 to $400 per trail day.

Full service on the trail

The price also covers a backpack, sleeping bag, sleeping pad and the food on the trail. Hotel nights before and after the trip are yours to book.

On this page: Four Season Guides, Discovery Treks, Wildland Trekking and Pygmy Guides, at $310 to $599 per trail day.

Full service with town nights

The trail part is full service. The hotel nights and dinners before and after the trail are in the price, or sold as a package beside it.

On this page: Dreamland, at $379 to $648 per trail day before the $300 town package, and Wildland Trekking's Coyote Gulch trip as sold through TrovaTrip.

What Dreamland's trail price covers

  • Experienced backpacking guides
  • Overnight permits
  • Trailhead transport from the closest town
  • Lightweight tents, team cooking equipment and water purification
  • Team first aid and emergency communication
  • Breakfasts, dinners and light lunches on the trail
  • A loaner backpack, sleeping bag and sleeping pad

The town package is $300 per person. It adds a hotel night and a team dinner before the trail and again after it, in the town where the trip starts, two guests to a room. It is optional. With it, the price is the same as the full trip price on our trip pages.

How we compare

  1. One unit. Price per person for each day on the trail. The trail days are printed beside every price.
  2. List prices only. We mention sales and never use them in a comparison.
  3. Every price links to the page we read it on, with the date we read it.
  4. The same columns for every outfitter: personal gear, trail meals, hotel nights and starting town.
  5. The rows where Dreamland costs more stay in.
  6. Corrections and a schedule. We read every source page again each month and review the whole page each January and July.

The small print

  • A trail day is a day spent hiking with a pack. Hotel days before and after a trip are not trail days.
  • Prices are per person, before sales tax, land-agency fees and guide tips. Outfitters add these in different ways.
  • Where an outfitter's page does not say whether something is in the price, the table says "Not stated".
  • Pygmy Guides publishes day rates. We computed its two trip totals from that rate card, and the tables show the sum.
  • Dreamland's prices are the 2027 prices.
