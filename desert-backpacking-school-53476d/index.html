A note from Kanab

Truth be told, you can do a lot of this without us.

What you can't do without is knowing where the water is, what the sky is telling you and when to turn around. We've guided out of Kanab since 2001, and this is what we tell our own guests before they shoulder a pack. Canyons change with every flood, so each page carries the date we last checked it. If you'd rather ask a person, call us at (801) 251-6036. You'll get our best advice even if you never book with us.

Sunny Stroeer, Paul Gagner and the Dreamland guides
