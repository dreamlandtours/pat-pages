Where we're going

Peekaboo Slot Canyon

Peekaboo is a twisting red-and-orange slot canyon a short drive north of Kanab. The road in is deep sand, so we take you out in our trucks. In the afternoon the sun hits the sculpted walls and they glow orange, which makes it a favorite for photos and for families. No climbing gear needed.

Have friends or family in town on another day? Our regular Peekaboo tour and the rest of our Kanab tours are on dreamlandtours.net.
