Where we're going

Peekaboo Slot Canyon

Peekaboo is a twisting red-and-orange slot canyon a short drive north of Kanab, carved out of Navajo sandstone. In the afternoon the sun hits the sculpted walls and they glow orange, which makes it a favorite for photos and for families. Keep an eye out for the Moqui steps, small notches cut into the walls about 1,000 years ago.

  • The walkAn easy 1-mile round trip on sand, rated 2 out of 10, with one short ladder. Closed, comfortable shoes are all you need.
  • The rideComfortable, climate-controlled, enclosed 4x4 Toyota Sequoias or Suburbans, with your guide at the wheel. No UTVs.
  • The timeAbout 3 hours, from our office and back: 2 to 5 PM.
  • DogsOur tours are dog-friendly. Your dog is welcome if it's well behaved and gets along with other dogs, since other guests may bring theirs too.

Getting there

From our office to the slot

Everyone meets at our office at 406 E 300 S in Kanab by 1:45 PM, and once we've checked IDs we head north out of town.

The last stretch into Peekaboo is some of the deepest, finest sand of any of our tours, which is why we do the driving in our capable trucks. We'll have you back at the office around 5 PM.

Good to know

Questions locals ask

Who counts as a Kane County local?

Anyone whose ID shows a Kane County address: Kanab, Big Water, Orderville, Glendale, Alton, Duck Creek and everywhere else in the county. Every adult in your group shows their ID when you check in at the office.

Can I bring friends or family who live somewhere else?

Yes. Book them along with your group and leave a note under Special requirements when you book. We'll charge the difference to our regular Peekaboo price ($119 for adults, $99 for kids 14 and under, plus tax and fees) when you check in.

How many people go on each trip?

Up to 10 guests, with 2 guides.

How hard is the walk?

It's an easy 1-mile round trip on sand, rated 2 out of 10, with one short ladder. Closed, comfortable shoes are all you need.

What will we ride in?

Comfortable, climate-controlled, enclosed 4x4 Toyota Sequoias or Suburbans, with your guide driving. We don't use UTVs.

How long is it?

About 3 hours. We leave from our office at 2 PM and have you back around 5.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Our tours are dog-friendly, so your dog is welcome if it's well behaved and gets along with other dogs. Other guests may bring theirs too, so a Sunday trip can have more than one dog along.

What if I'd rather not have dogs on my trip?

Tell us under Special requirements when you book. We'll see what we can do, but on these Sunday trips we can't guarantee it. If you need a trip without dogs for sure, book one of our regular tours and let us know: on those we can guarantee it.

Do kids pay?

Kids 2 and under ride free. Everyone else from Kane County is $25 plus tax and fees.

What if I need to cancel, or the weather turns?

The same cancellation and weather policy as all our tours applies.

Why we're doing this

Twenty-five years in Kanab

Dreamland turned 25 this year. We spent November celebrating with community hikes, two speaker nights and a party at the Best Friends Roadhouse, and a lot of you came out for it. Now that winter has things a bit quieter around here, we're stoked we can open up these Sunday afternoons to our neighbors.

Before Paul and Sunny bought Dreamland in 2020, they were living in a van, and they went out on a Dreamland tour as guests to see what they'd be buying. Orion guided that trip and Andrea helped with the permits. Both still guide for Dreamland today.

Today it's Paul, Sunny and a crew of guides heading out from our office at 406 E 300 S nearly every day of the year. Stop in and say hi.
