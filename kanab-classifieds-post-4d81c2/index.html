Kanab locals! Just about everyone here has heard of Peekaboo, but the deep sand on the way in keeps a lot of us from ever getting out there. So on Sunday afternoons we're taking Kane County folks out in our trucks, for $25 a person plus tax and fees, and kids 2 and under ride free. Sundays at 2 PM: Nov 29, Dec 6, Dec 13 and Dec 20. We keep each trip to 10 guests with 2 guides, so the earlier Sundays may fill up. Everyone meets at our office at 406 E 300 S, and every adult shows an ID with a Kane County address. Family in town for the holidays is welcome to come along at our regular Peekaboo price. Details and booking: 25years.dreamlandtours.net/community Happy Thanksgiving from all of us at Dreamland!
